About TerraScope

TerraScope is an open-source global Earth observation analytics dashboard engineered for the NASA Space Apps / Global Challenge. It detects, evaluates, and communicates secular climate trends across countries, states, districts, and arbitrary point coordinates worldwide.

Authoritative Earth Observation Sources

  • NASA POWER Project: Meteorological records for near-surface air temperature (T2M), precipitation (PRECTOTCORR), and soil wetness (GWETTOP, GWETROOT) on a 0.5° grid (1981–present).
  • GPM IMERG: Global Precipitation Measurement satellite precipitation records.
  • SWOT Mission Proxy: Surface water extent and reservoir elevation indicators.
  • geoBoundaries Open Database: Global administrative boundaries across Country (ADM0), State/Province (ADM1), and District/County (ADM2) tiers (Runfola et al., CC-BY 4.0 / ODbL).
  • Maps.co & Nominatim: Dynamic worldwide forward and reverse geocoding API.

Scientific Rigour & Statistics

Rather than relying on ordinary least squares regression, TerraScope applies non-parametric Mann-Kendall monotonic trend tests paired with Sen's robust slope estimators and 95% confidence intervals. Every quantitative calculation is translated into an accessible plain-language narrative.
