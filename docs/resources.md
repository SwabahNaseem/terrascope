# External Resources, References & Validation Sources

## 1. NASA & Earth Observation Platforms
- **NASA POWER (Prediction of Worldwide Energy Resources)**:
  - URL: `https://power.larc.nasa.gov/`
  - Documentation: `https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/`
- **NASA Giovanni (GES DISC)**:
  - URL: `https://giovanni.gsfc.nasa.gov/giovanni/`
  - Role: Validation portal for monthly gridded time series comparison.
- **NASA Data Rods for Hydrology**:
  - Role: Direct GLDAS time-series extraction for cross-checking soil moisture trends.
- **AppEEARS (Application for Extracting and Exploring Analysis Ready Samples)**:
  - Role: Point and area data subsetting for Land Processes DAAC datasets.

## 2. Statistical Methodology References
- **Mann, H. B. (1945)**: Nonparametric tests against trend. *Econometrica*, 13, 245–259.
- **Kendall, M. G. (1975)**: *Rank Correlation Methods*. Charles Griffin, London.
- **Sen, P. K. (1968)**: Estimates of the regression coefficient based on Kendall's tau. *Journal of the American Statistical Association*, 63(324), 1379–1389.
- **Yue, S., & Wang, C. Y. (2004)**: The Mann-Kendall test modified by effective sample size to detect trend in serially correlated hydrological series. *Water Resources Management*, 18(3), 201–218.

## 3. Climate Reference Platforms
- **KNMI Climate Explorer**:
  - URL: `https://climexp.knmi.nl/`
  - Role: Independent peer verification of trend directions and p-values for peninsular and northern India regions.
