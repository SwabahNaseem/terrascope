About TerraScope

TerraScope: Earth System Trend Detective (India Edition) is an interactive analytical platform engineered to reveal long-term environmental trends across the Indian subcontinent using satellite assimilation data.

Data Sources & Spaceborne Observations

Data is sourced directly from NASA Earth Science systems, including the NASA POWER Project (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources) utilizing MERRA-2 and GEOS assimilation products at 0.5° spatial resolution, corroborated against GPM IMERG precipitation and GLDAS land surface models.

Statistical Methodology

Trends are assessed using the non-parametric Mann-Kendall test, which does not assume a normal distribution and is robust against outliers and missing data. Rate of change is quantified using Sen's Slope Estimator. Results with \(p < 0.05\) are classified as statistically significant secular changes.

Scientific Responsibility Disclaimer

Statistical trend detection demonstrates monotonic association over the selected observation window but does not alone establish direct anthropogenic causality. Local microclimates, urban heat island effects, and regional topography (such as the Western Ghats) introduce spatial assimilation uncertainties.
